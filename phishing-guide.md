# Phishing Awareness Guide

## What is Phishing
Phishing is a type of cyber attack where an attacker senda fake emails, sms, or textx pretending to be from a trusted organization or company like banks to trick individuals into giving their credentials such as passwords or rather personal data
Many times common phishing may be fake bank alerts, fake job alerts or fake money offers.

## Types Of Phishing
1.Spear Phishing-this type of phishing is targeted at certain or specific people.

2.Vishing-Phishing via phone call

3.Email Phishing-Fake email messages accompanied by malicious links

## How To Identify Phishing
### Urgency
"Your account will be closed in 2 hours! Take action now!".
IT messages or trusted messages from companies do not threaten or have a need for urgency.

### Suspicious Sender Address
Fake: fnb-support@fnb-secure-login.com or fnb123@gmail.com
Real: support@fnb.co.bw

### Mismatched Links
Where by the email says www.fnb.co.bw but link shows http://bit.ly/xxyz436, its phishing.
